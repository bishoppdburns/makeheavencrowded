The Vision

Why Now. Why Here. Why Us.

Whitfield and Murray Counties sit at a crossroads. Hundreds of thousands of people within a 30-mile radius, many of whom are walking through life without Christ. The opioid crisis, family fracture, poverty, and spiritual emptiness are visible on every street corner. The Church has a choice.

“I say to you that likewise there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine just persons who need no repentance.”

Luke 15:7 (NKJV)

This movement is built on one conviction: eternity is at stake. Every soul in Dalton, Chatsworth, and the surrounding communities is a person Jesus died for. This is not a one-church campaign. It is a unified, region-wide effort — churches, ministries, and community organizations joining forces under a single Kingdom banner.

We do not compete. We complete. Every church that joins this movement is a partner in the greatest cause on earth.

5 Core Initiatives

How We Move Together

Movement partners choose which initiatives to host or participate in based on their capacity and calling. You do not have to do everything — but you must do something.

01
The 30-Day Church Challenge
Every movement church runs a unified 30-day devotional campaign simultaneously using the Make Heaven Crowded devotional. When the whole region reads and prays the same thing, the Spirit moves region-wide.
Church-Wide
02
Neighborhood Blitz Outreach Days
Coordinated, city-wide outreach Saturdays where movement churches deploy teams into Dalton, Chatsworth, and surrounding neighborhoods simultaneously. No single church carries the load.
Quarterly
03
Make Heaven Crowded Crusade Night
A large-scale, open-air evangelism event with the full weight of the movement behind it. Worship, testimonies, a clear Gospel invitation, and a goal of 1,000+ in attendance with 100+ documented salvations.
Annual Event
04
Marketplace Evangelism Drive
Whitfield County is the carpet capital of the world. Tens of thousands of workers go to their jobs every week without Christ. This initiative equips believers to carry the Gospel into factories, offices, and break rooms.
Workplace
05
Recovery Community Integration
Northwest Georgia has been devastated by life-controlling issues. The Breakthrough Recovery program positions the Church as the answer — not just a referral source. Every movement church is equipped to reach this community.

Launch Timeline

90-Day Movement Plan

The first 90 days are the foundation. Move fast, build momentum, and let the Spirit set the pace.

Phase 1 • Weeks 1-2
Movement Formation
Personal outreach calls to pastors, host the Founding Partner dinner, and distribute movement commitment kits across both counties.
Phase 2 • Weeks 3-4
Movement Kickoff Service
Make Heaven Crowded Movement Kickoff Service hosted at Relentless Worship Center. Book launch, vision casting, public commitment, and corporate prayer over the region.
Phase 3 • Weeks 5-8
30-Day Church Challenge
Church-wide devotional campaign launches simultaneously across all movement churches. #MakeHeavenCrowded social media push begins with daily content.
Phase 4 • Weeks 7-9
First Neighborhood Blitz Day
All movement zones deploy simultaneously across Whitfield and Murray Counties. Prayer, outreach, book distribution, and on-the-spot Gospel conversations.
Phase 5 • Weeks 10-12
Assessment & Crusade Prep
Track salvations, celebrate testimonies, confirm Crusade Night venue and date, and begin building the full event volunteer team.
