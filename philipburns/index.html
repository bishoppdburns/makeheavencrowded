Meet Philip

He preaches like eternity is real.

Because it is.

Philip D. Burns is the pastor of Relentless Worship Center in Dalton, Georgia, and the founder of Relentless School of Ministry, Relentless Ministry Network and Breakthrough Recovery. He carries a burden for the lost, a fire for revival and a practical gift for building churches that are healthy, organized and full of the Holy Spirit's power.

Whether he is standing behind a pulpit, sitting across from a pastor who is worn out, or praying with someone fighting for their freedom, the message is the same. Jesus saves. The Holy Spirit empowers. And every believer has a part in filling Heaven.
