Questions

Host FAQ

How far in advance should we invite Philip?

Three to six months is ideal, especially for conferences and multi-night revivals. Shorter notice is sometimes possible, so ask anyway.

What about honorarium and travel?

Every invitation is prayed over, not priced out. Hosts typically cover travel and lodging and provide an honorarium or love offering. If budget is a concern for a smaller church, don't let that stop you from reaching out.

Can Philip bring a team?

Yes. For revivals and outreach weekends, Philip can bring members of his ministry team to help with altar ministry, prayer and evangelism training.

Will there be a book table?

With your permission, we bring copies of Make Heaven Crowded and the 30-Day Devotional. Churches can also pre-order at bulk pricing to put a copy in every member's hands.

Do you do virtual events or podcasts?

Yes. Philip is available for podcast interviews, virtual trainings and online gatherings. Choose “Podcast, interview or media” in the form.
