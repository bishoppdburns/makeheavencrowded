Why Breakthrough

The Church is the answer. Not just a referral.

Addiction and bondage don't just break individuals. They break families, churches and whole communities.

Breakthrough Recovery was born out of a conviction that the local church should be the safest, strongest place in town for someone fighting to get free. It combines the honesty of a recovery group with the power of the Gospel and the structure of real discipleship, so people don't just stop something. They start following Jesus.

“The Spirit of the Lord is upon Me... He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives.”Luke 4:18 NKJV

Who It's For

Whatever the chain, Jesus breaks it.

Addiction

Drugs, alcohol and every substance that promised relief and delivered a prison.

Hidden Bondage

The battles nobody sees. Pornography, gambling, anger and secret sin that thrives in the dark.

Wounds & Trauma

Grief, abuse and the pain underneath the behavior, brought into the light and healed.

Families

Parents, spouses and children who love someone still in the fight and need hope of their own.
