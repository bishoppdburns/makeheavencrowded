The Book

Make Heaven Crowded

Reclaiming the urgency of evangelism and raising up disciples who multiply.

Somewhere along the way, much of the Church stopped expecting ordinary believers to win souls. Make Heaven Crowded is a call to change that. It lays a biblical foundation for evangelism, hands you practical tools for everyday witness, and casts a Kingdom vision for a Church that takes the Gospel into neighborhoods, workplaces, schools and recovery rooms.

This is not a book for the few with the gift of evangelism. It is a book for every believer, and every church, that is ready to stop watching and start reaching.
