His Story

Philip D. Burns is the pastor of Relentless Worship Center in Dalton, Georgia, a revival preacher, apostolic leader and the author of Make Heaven Crowded.

He is the founder of Relentless School of Ministry, Relentless Ministry Network and Breakthrough Recovery, a Christ-centered recovery and discipleship ministry. His preaching is marked by urgency, biblical depth and a deep dependence on the Holy Spirit, and his leadership is marked by something just as important: the practical wisdom to build churches that are healthy, organized and effective.

Philip believes the Church was never meant to be a museum for the saved. It was meant to be a rescue mission, full of power, full of love and full of people who know their part in the Great Commission. That conviction drives everything he does, from the pulpit to the recovery room to the leadership table.
